# Master Data Sync
Master Data Sync is a solution for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.  

This solution allows you to synchronize master data between different Business Central environments, ensuring consistency and accuracy across your organization.  

## Solution enables
* the synchronisation of master data between different Business Central environments (not just between companies within a single environment).
